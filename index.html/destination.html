Acatenango Volcano Overnight Trek

Guatemala | Extreme Level: 🌋🌋🌋🌋🌋

The trek up Volcán Acatenango is one of the most physically demanding and visually rewarding adventures in Central America. You will hike for 5-6 hours to base camp, where you spend the night watching its neighbor, **Volcán Fuego**, erupt every 10-20 minutes. This is not a casual stroll; it is a true test of endurance.

The Challenge:

You'll need an acceptable level of fitness for the trip.However,you don't need to have any climbing experience to enjoy the moment

Quick Book Info

Duration: 2 Days / 1 Night

Price From: $150 USD (incl. gear)

Requirement: High Fitness Level

Inclusions:

  • Certified Mountain Guide
  • Tents, Sleeping Bag, Warm Coats
  • Dinner and Breakfast
  • Park Fees and Transport
